I am **Junteng Liu**, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am a member of the **HKUST NLP Group** advised by **Professor Junxian He**. I received my B.Eng. from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor He.

My research lies at the intersection of **natural language processing** and **machine learning**. I am particularly interested in:

- Large language model reasoning and reinforcement learning
- Hallucination in vision-language models
- LLM truthfulness and interpretability

## News and current work
